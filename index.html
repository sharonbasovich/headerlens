A DELIBERATELY SMALL LENS

Clear boundaries, not a score.

HeaderLens explains a correction to simple tables. It refuses merged or multilevel headers, nested tables, rich content and custom association systems instead of guessing.

Missing explicit scope does not mean missing accessibility. Browsers may infer associations. This tool does not reproduce a screen reader, certify WCAG compliance or replace testing in context.
